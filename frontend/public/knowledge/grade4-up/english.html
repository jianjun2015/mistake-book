Module 1: My neighbourhood

Unit 1: Where's the school?

核心词汇

单词音标词性中文
school/skuːl/n.学校
hospital/ˈhɒspɪtl/n.医院
park/pɑːk/n.公园

核心句型

Module 3: Shopping

Unit 5: How much is it?

核心词汇

单词音标词性中文
how much/haʊ mʌtʃ/adv.多少钱
cheap/tʃiːp/adj.便宜的
expensive/ɪkˈspensɪv/adj.贵的
yuan/juˈɑːn/n.元

核心句型

A: How much is the book?

B: It's 20 yuan.

📝 语法总结

1. 特殊疑问句

Where — 哪里：Where's the school?
What — 什么：What's the weather like?
How much — 多少钱：How much is it?

2. 形容词

描述天气：sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, snowy
描述价格：cheap, expensive

📝 本学期重点

必背单词

地点：school, hospital, park, library

天气：sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, snowy

方位：next to, between

必背句型

  • Where's the hospital? — It's next to the park.
  • What's the weather like? — It's sunny.
  • How much is the book? — It's 20 yuan.
