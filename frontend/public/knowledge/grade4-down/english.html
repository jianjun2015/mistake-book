📝 语法总结

1. 时间表达

整点：It's 7 o'clock.
半点：It's half past 7.
非整点：It's 7:30.

2. 一般现在时

肯定句：I usually play football.
否定句：I don't play football.
疑问句：Do you play football?

📝 本学期重点

必背单词

房间：bedroom, living room, kitchen, bathroom

时间：get up, go to school, go to bed

星期：Monday - Sunday

餐食：breakfast, lunch, dinner

必背句型

  • Where's the cat? — It's in the kitchen.
  • What time is it? — It's 7 o'clock.
  • What do you do on weekends? — I usually play football.
