📝 语法总结

1. be 动词的用法

I am (I'm) — 我是
He/She/It is (He's/She's/It's) — 他/她/它是
You/We/They are (You're/We're/They're) — 你/我们/他们是

2. 特殊疑问句

What — 什么：What's your name?
Where — 哪里：Where is my book?

📝 本学期重点

必背单词

家庭成员：father, mother, brother, sister

职业：doctor, teacher, student

方位：in, on, under

物品：desk, chair, book, ruler

必背句型

  • Hello! How are you? — I'm fine, thank you.
  • What's your name? — My name is Tom.
  • What does your father do? — My father is a doctor.
  • Where is my ruler? — It's on the desk.
